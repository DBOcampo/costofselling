What these numbers include

Mortgage payoff: A mortgage payoff is different from the mortgage balance. The payoff estimate is based on information supplied by the seller. Verify the correct amount with your mortgage company.

Estimated taxes: Use tax information reported on the county website. This estimate includes 21 months of taxes, entered as a dollar total. If you escrow for taxes, the original sheet notes that your remaining escrow balance is refunded by your mortgage company approximately four weeks after closing. Confirm the amount and timing with your lender; that refund is not added to the seller net here.

Items shaded gray: Negotiable items on the purchase agreement. These vary by offer and negotiations. Commission amounts depend on the applicable signed agreements.

Estimate only: These figures are approximate and based on the information provided. They are not final calculations. Actual proceeds depend on the final contract, lender payoff statements and settlement figures. We make no guarantees and are not responsible for differences from these estimates on your final closing statement.
